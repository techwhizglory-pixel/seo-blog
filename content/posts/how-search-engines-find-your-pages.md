Search engines work in three stages: they **crawl** pages, **index** what they find, and **rank** results for each query.

## Crawling

Bots follow links from page to page. If no link points to a page, it may never be found. A `sitemap.xml` helps, but internal links matter more.

## Indexing

After crawling, the engine decides whether the page is worth storing. Thin, duplicate or blocked pages often get left out. Use a canonical URL to say which version of a page is the main one.

## Ranking

Ranking weighs relevance, quality and usability. Write for a specific question, answer it clearly, and keep the page fast.

## What to do next

Submit your sitemap in Google Search Console, then check the Pages report to see what was indexed and why anything wasn't.
