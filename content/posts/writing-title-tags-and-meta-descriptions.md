Your title tag and meta description are the first thing a searcher sees. They rarely change rankings directly, but they strongly affect whether someone clicks.

## Title tags

Keep titles under about 60 characters so they aren't cut off. Put the main topic first and make each title unique across the site.

## Meta descriptions

Aim for 120 to 155 characters. Describe what the reader will get, in plain words. Google may rewrite your description, so make the page content support it too.

## Test and learn

Change one title at a time, wait a few weeks, then compare click-through rate in Search Console. Keep notes on what you changed and why.
